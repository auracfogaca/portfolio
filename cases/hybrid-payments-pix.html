Market context

02 — the problem

Customers don't think in payment methods.

A customer wants to pay their electricity bill. They open the app and face a question: Pix? TED? TEF? Find the utility's website directly? Each path requires different steps, different confirmations, different anxiety about whether the money reached the right place.

Customers think in jobs: "Pay my electricity bill safely" or "Send money to my mother quickly." Yet the app forced them to navigate a taxonomy of legacy payment rails — each with its own flow.

Job-to-Be-Done

When I need to pay a utility bill or send money, I want to do it quickly, safely, and without worrying about which method to use. I want to stay organised and confident that my financial obligations are up to date.

03 — approach

Four design decisions that unified the experience

DECISION_01

Unified entry point

Eliminated the choice architecture. Instead of "which payment method?" — one input: who are you paying, how much, when. The app determines the optimal rail (Pix P2P, TED B2B, auto-billing for utilities). No decision required.

DECISION_02

Multimodal intelligence

Three input modes: camera (scan a bill, extract amount + recipient), voice ("pay my electricity bill"), text (natural language). A customer could say "pay my electricity" and the app finds the account and prepares payment.

DECISION_03

AI-driven automation

ML detected patterns — rent paid every 1st, shared meals every Friday. The system anticipated payments, not just executed them. 90% of users trusted AI enough to automate at least one recurring payment.

DECISION_04

Safety-first design

More automation = more risk. We implemented fraud detection (flagging unusual amounts/recipients), payment verification for new payees, and automated refund via Central Bank's Pix reversal mechanism (90-day window).

06 — before → after

What changed in the experience

BEFORE

Fragmented payment landscape

Separate flows for Pix, TED, TEF, utility billing, subscriptions. Each method had different confirmations. No automation, no intelligence. Every payment was a manual decision.

AFTER

Unified intelligent journey

Single payment entry. AI suggests payment method automatically. Camera, voice, and text inputs. Recurring payments detected and offered for automation. Split bills (Cofrinho/Pay Together) enabled. 55% adoption of shared payments.

08 — what comes next

Building the future of payments

Jun 2025

Pix Automatic

Central Bank approved Pix recurring payments. Businesses collect recurring bills (utilities, subscriptions) over the Pix rail — across different banks. Our journey auto-detects recurring bills and offers automation.

Feb 2025

Pix NFC (Contactless)

Tap-to-pay via NFC — Pix goes in-person retail. Competitive with Apple Pay and Visa Contactless, but lower merchant fees. Our 30M+ active digital users were ready for this.

2025

BNPL + Pix Integration

Buy-now-pay-later via Pix. Customers split purchases into 2–12 Pix installments instead of credit card. Lower friction for customers, lower cost for merchants.

2025–2026

Predictive automation + International expansion

ML proactively suggests payment schedules from spending patterns. Leveraging Itaú's regional presence for cross-border Pix in Colombia (Q4 2025) and Mexico (2026).

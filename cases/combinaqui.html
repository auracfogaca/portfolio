02 — overview

From aggregation to integration

When Combinaqui launched in 2023, the ambition was clear: empower people to improve their financial well-being beyond traditional banking products. But the initial version missed something fundamental. We were aggregating services — not solving the underlying problem.

Customers didn't want more products. They wanted the right combination — accessible, simple, and meaningful to their actual goals. Our 40+ million user base gave us a massive opportunity. The 2025 redesign solved it.

03 — the problem

Eleven subscriptions. Zero coherence.

A customer opens their banking app and sees eleven separate subscriptions across streaming, insurance, and utilities. Each with a different login, different interface, different billing date. The experience is fragmented, exhausting, and impossible to optimise.

Core insight

When people want to improve their financial well-being, they want an integrated platform that combines offers in one place — so they can make smarter choices, save money, and access solutions that fit their lifestyle.

04 — approach

Three shifts that changed everything

SHIFT_01
Job-to-Be-Done reframe
Stopped asking "How do we show more products?" Started asking "What job does the customer want done?" Answer: simplify subscription chaos into a single, personalised decision.
SHIFT_02
Content-first architecture
Redesigned the product page around stories, not feature lists. Why cashback? Because freedom of choice generates savings. Why combine? Because unified management saves time. Structure aligned to the job.
SHIFT_03
Real-time activation control
Added visibility into the activation process. Customers see their status in real time — no mystery, no waiting, no support tickets. Manage services wherever they prefer.
05 — experience changes

What changed in the journey

BEFORE

Static product grid

Customers saw 11+ services listed individually. Checkout was multi-step with unclear value proposition. Savings were invisible, combinations were manual, payment was single-rail (direct debit only).

AFTER

Interactive combo builder

Feature discovery through story modules. Intelligent combo recommendations based on product affinity. Clear savings highlighted upfront (up to 87%). Reduced checkout friction. Credit card + BNPL + Pix Parcelado added.

+9% SUM improvement

Based on ~200 guerrilla test respondents. The improvement over the previous journey validated our content-first approach — customers felt the experience was effortless and understood the value immediately.

07 — next phase

Building the ecosystem: 2025–2026

Globoplay proved the model works. Now we're scaling systematically across payment methods, partner categories, and user segments.

PILLAR_01

Payment method expansion

Direct debit is reliable but reaches only account holders. We're adding:

  • Credit card (41% of Brazil's online transactions)
  • BNPL (14% YoY growth, USD 4.66B market)
  • Pix Parcelado (Central Bank, Sep 2025)
PILLAR_02

Partner ecosystem scale

Globoplay is one. Targeting 5–7 new major partners quarterly:

  • Streaming (proven model)
  • Insurance (high-margin recurring)
  • Utilities (new category, high frequency)
  • Fintech integrations
PILLAR_03

Lifetime value optimisation

We know which combo each customer chose. Now we personalise:

  • AI-driven recommendations by usage patterns
  • Upgrade/downgrade pathways (not just cancel)
  • Target: −12% 6-month churn
PILLAR_04

International expansion

Itaú's regional presence creates Latin America opportunity:

  • Colombia pilot — Q4 2025
  • Mexico expansion — 2026
  • Adapted payment rails, same JTBD

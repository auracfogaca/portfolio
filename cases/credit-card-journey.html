02 — overview

From fragmented to unified

Itaú's credit card business underwent comprehensive redesign as part of a broader digital transformation. The focus: simplify the four moments that define card ownership — applying for one, receiving it, activating it, and using it every day.

Strategy grounded in deep customer understanding: 4,000+ hours of research, 1,600+ corporate customers interviewed. Priority journeys identified by frequency of use, satisfaction scores, and support call volume.

03 — execution

Three phases, two years

2023

Design System & Navigation Architecture

Developed Itaú Design Language (IDL) ensuring consistency across physical and digital channels. Redesigned app navigation prioritising journeys by frequency of use and cognitive effort. Built reusable component library for scalable growth.

2023–2024

Core App Redesign & Cards Hub Launch

Simplified card discovery, onboarding, and activation. Launched Cards Hub consolidating management of multiple cards. Implemented first-screen prioritisation (80% of features accessible on home). Introduced split payments and Pix Credit features.

2024–2025

Superapp Migration & AI Integration

Migrated 15 million single-product users from legacy apps to unified superapp. Introduced AI-driven transaction insights, personalised recommendations, and predictive alerts. Enabled context-aware experience adapting to each user's profile at login.

07 — growth strategy

Design as CAC reduction

The redesign directly addresses customer acquisition cost through three mechanisms — each translating design decisions into financial outcomes.

LEVER_01

Streamlined digital onboarding

Eliminated friction from acquisition journey. Smart pre-fill reduced form fields. 21% speed improvement = faster conversion, lower cart abandonment. More customers per marketing peso spent.

LEVER_02

Activation velocity

80% of features on first screen = customers use the card faster. Faster first transaction = stronger habit formation = lower churn. Lower churn = higher LTV per acquisition dollar.

LEVER_03

Multi-product cross-sell

Superapp consolidation enables recommendation of complementary products. Cross-sell reduces blended CAC across the bundle. Premium segment (20.2% growth) validates the upsell potential.

net effect

Lower CAC through design efficiency + higher revenue per customer through volume and premium growth + reduced churn = improved unit economics. 2024 delivered 14% volume lift and 20.2% premium growth simultaneously.

01About

// who & how

I design consumer products for scale — fintech flows trusted by 40M+ users, AI-native tools that eliminate manual work, and ecosystems that turn complexity into decisions people make in seconds.

Over the past decade I've grown NPS by +16 points, improved usability metrics by +28%, and driven +30% engagement — through rigorous, evidence-based continuous discovery. I've shipped at Itaú, Cora, and across Brazil's fastest-moving fintechs.

I work end-to-end: qualitative research, journey mapping, system architecture, WCAG-compliant design, and post-launch measurement. I embed quality at the process level, mentor design teams, and bridge product, engineering, and data science — turning complex systems into trustworthy, human experiences.

Currently deepening this practice through AI-driven product design at MIT Professional Education — building the skills to design what comes next.

TINKERING /ˈtɪŋ.kər.ɪŋ/  ·  noun

An iterative, hands-on, playful approach to creation — thinking with your hands. It starts with materials, data investigations or a concept, and discovers possibilities through playful manipulation — learning through dead-ends and failures.
